### Motivator Buckets
Score is 0 to 5, one point per bucket. Rank order below is also the column order.

1. **Title progression** (carried in the Title cell): ✓ when the title is a rung above Director (Senior Director, VP, Head of with VP scope). ✗ for a same-level Director title [DL-B1]
2. **Pay clears floor:** ✓ when the posted low-end base (or base plus public RSUs) is at least $185,000. ✗ when no pay is posted
3. **Commute / remote:** ✓ when fully remote, or hybrid with a rush-hour drive of 40 minutes or less. ✗ otherwise
4. **Stability:** ✓ when public signals show no layoff in the past 12 months and funding or profit to cover the next two years. ✗ otherwise
5. **Reviews:** ✓ when the employer rates 3.0 or higher on both Glassdoor and Indeed. ✗ when either is under 3.0, or neither has a rating

### Rank Overrides
Applied after the score, as bands:

1. A role with no posted pay ranks below every role that clears the floor on a stated number, whatever its score
2. Anything besides full-time would rank a band below full-time (Sam chose full-time only, so this is dormant)
3. Ties break on comp, higher first, undisclosed lowest
